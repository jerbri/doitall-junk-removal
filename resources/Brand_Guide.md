# Do It All Junk Removal: Brand Guide

## Overview

**Do It All Junk Removal** is a local, two-person junk hauling crew (Ryan & Jacob). You point at it, they load it, haul it, and leave the space swept. No job too small, no item too awkward.

The brand should feel **loud, local, and hardworking**: a lifted black truck, a mountain of junk, neon green paint. It is not corporate and should never sound like it.

---

## Name

**Do It All Junk Removal**

- Short form: **Do It All Junk** or **Do It All**
- Always "Do It All" (three words, title case). Never "DoItAll" in prose (the email handle is the only exception).

---

## Tagline

**You point. We haul.**

Supporting lines:
- Residential • Commercial • Cleanouts
- If it fits on the truck, it's gone.
- Text a pic. Get a price. Done.

---

## Voice

- **Straight talk:** short sentences, plain words. "We'll haul it today" beats "We offer expedited removal solutions."
- **Confident, not cocky:** they show up, work hard, and the price doesn't change.
- **Friendly and local:** first names, "we," "you." Ryan & Jacob are the brand.
- **No fluff:** no buzzwords, no "solutions," no "synergy."

---

## Logo

Source: [`logo.jpg`](logo.jpg) (1254 x 1254, black background).

- Always place on a black or near-black background. The badge is designed for dark.
- Keep clear space of at least 1/10 of the badge width on all sides.
- Do not recolor, stretch, crop the circle, or add drop shadows.
- Minimum display size: 64px (favicon), 120px in layouts.
- The phone numbers baked into the badge are legacy; the website and print use the numbers in the site config.

Web exports live in `site/src/assets/` and `site/public/` (favicon, apple-touch icon, social share image).

## Color Palette

Sampled from the logo.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Junk Green | `#8CF50A` | Primary accent, buttons, highlights, icons |
| Green Deep | `#5FB800` | Hover/pressed states, borders on dark |
| Black | `#0A0A0A` | Page background |
| Truck Black | `#151515` | Cards, panels |
| Steel | `#262626` | Borders, dividers |
| White | `#F5F5F5` | Headlines, primary text on dark |
| Concrete | `#A3A3A3` | Body copy, secondary text |

Rules:
- Dark backgrounds always. The site is a black page with neon green punches.
- Green on black or black on green. Never green on white (fails contrast).
- Use green sparingly: CTAs, icons, one highlighted word per headline.

## Typography

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headlines | **Anton** | Condensed, all caps, echoes "JUNK REMOVAL" in the logo |
| Accent | **Permanent Marker** | Echoes the brush "DO IT ALL". Use for one or two words max (e.g., "Ryan & Jacob") |
| Body / UI | **Inter** | 400 to 800 weights |

All from Google Fonts.

## Imagery

- Real photos of real jobs beat stock photos every time. Before/after shots are gold.
- Pull from their TikTok and Facebook: truck loaded, empty garage after, the two of them working.
- Avoid generic stock "smiling movers."

---

## Services

- **Furniture:** couches, mattresses, dressers, recliners
- **Appliances:** fridges, washers, dryers, water heaters
- **Cleanouts:** garages, attics, sheds, estates, rentals, storage units
- **Yard debris:** branches, brush, fencing, old playsets
- **Construction debris:** drywall, flooring, cabinets, small reno leftovers
- **Commercial:** office furniture, retail fixtures, property turnovers
- **Hot tubs, sheds & teardowns:** light demo and haul-off
- **Anything else:** if two guys and a trailer can move it, ask
